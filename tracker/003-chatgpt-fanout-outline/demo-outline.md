**H1: How to Get Your Business Recommended by ChatGPT (2026 Guide for Local and Service Businesses)**

**Quick answer (first 50 words, for AI to quote):** ChatGPT recommends businesses it sees described
consistently and positively across the web: review sites, directories, comparison articles and your own
clear service pages. There is no form to submit. You earn it with entity clarity, reviews, third-party
mentions and pages that answer buyer questions directly.

- **H2: Why AI recommendations matter now**
  - H3: Buyers ask ChatGPT "best X near me" instead of Google
  - H3: Recommendation vs citation: being *suggested* is different from being *quoted*
- **H2: How ChatGPT finds and chooses businesses**
  - H3: It rewrites your question into several web searches ("fan-out queries")
  - H3: It reads the top results, review platforms and listicles for those searches
  - H3: Make sure OpenAI's search crawler (OAI-SearchBot) is not blocked in robots.txt
- **H2: 7 ways to get recommended**
  - H3: 1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile (and JustDial / Sulekha for India)
  - H3: 2. Get into "best of" and comparison articles in your category
  - H3: 3. Build reviews on the platforms your buyers use
  - H3: 4. Keep name, address, phone and services identical everywhere (entity clarity)
  - H3: 5. Publish buyer-question pages: cost, comparisons, "is it worth it", problems
  - H3: 6. Use clear, structured pages: FAQ blocks, schema, one topic per page
  - H3: 7. Earn third-party mentions that use your exact category words
- **H2: What does not work**
  - H3: Stuffing "ChatGPT" keywords, buying fake reviews, AI-spun mass content
- **H2: Test it yourself in 15 minutes** *(unique section)*
  - H3: Ask ChatGPT your 5 buyer questions with search on
  - H3: Open the browser's Network tab to see the exact searches it ran
  - H3: Check who ranks for those searches, and close the gaps
- **H2: How to measure and stay visible**
  - H3: Re-test monthly; AI answers change often
- **H2: FAQ**
  - Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT? · How long does it take? · Does Google SEO still matter?
